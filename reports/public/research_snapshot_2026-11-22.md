# Research Snapshot: Gate 2 with the Hardened PPO Baseline

Date: 2026-11-22

Status: diagnostic. Generated by
`python examples/run_gate2.py --seeds 5 --ppo-config configs/gridworld_v2_ppo_hardened.yaml`
(schema `gate2/v4`, seeds 0-4, run one seed per process and combined).

## Result

Gate 2 still fails. Two things changed, and neither is a gain for the
energy method.

1. **The hardened PPO is more exploitable, not less.** It reached the goal in
   training on 4 of 5 seeds, and its exact exploitability rose from 1.598 to
   1.768. That is worse than the uniform random mixture (1.633).
2. **The EBM mixture row is not reproducible across runs.** The trained
   energy model is the same, but the row moved on every seed, by up to 0.26.

## Reading it

- **Why a better PPO scores worse.** Exploitability is measured against an
  exact best-responding defender. The 4,096-step policy is close to uniform
  over actions, which is hard to exploit. The hardened policy commits to one
  route that beats one scripted defender, and a best response intercepts it.
  Solving the training task and being robust are different targets here.
- **"EBM beats PPO" flipped for the wrong reason.** The EBM row did not
  improve; PPO got worse. It should not be reported as a win.
- **Why the EBM row moved.** `_ebm_mixture` draws its Langevin samples from
  the global random state, and PPO training runs just before it. A different
  PPO budget leaves a different random state, so the mixture is built from
  different samples of the same energy model. Per seed:

  | Seed | Default PPO run | Hardened PPO run |
  | ---: | ---: | ---: |
  | 0 | 1.507 | 1.651 |
  | 1 | 1.534 | 1.635 |
  | 2 | 1.671 | 1.592 |
  | 3 | 1.515 | 1.565 |
  | 4 | 1.639 | 1.380 |

  The spread from the sampling draw alone is as large as the gap between the
  EBM row and the baselines. The "beats diversity-only" flag flipped because
  of it, so that flag was never stable.

## Caveats

- Five seeds, one draw of the mixture per seed per run. The sampling variance
  is seen, not measured.
- The hardened PPO trains against one scripted defender. PPO trained against
  a mix of defenders or by self-play has not been run and is the fairer
  robust baseline.

## Next

- Seed the mixture sampling independently of the other baselines, and report
  the EBM row as a mean over many draws per trained model. This changes the
  headline EBM figure in the paper, so it needs a decision before it is done.
- Report both PPO baselines in the paper table, or state plainly that the
  PPO row is a near-uniform 4,096-step policy.
- PPO against a mix of scripted defenders.
